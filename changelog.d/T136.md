- A worker spawn refuses a task that a live reservation holds, naming the holder. A reservation whose monitor and harness process group have both exited is recorded as cleared under the state lock, so it no longer holds a worker slot.
- A supervised worker's harness starts only after its supervisor takes the worker's lease, so no worktree write precedes the lease on any platform. A worker whose lease is refused or cannot be taken stops with `no lease` and exits non-zero. The agent's own claim renews that lease, and a worker's exit leaves its lease in place until it is released. `supervision.claim_ms` is removed; a rung that still sets it is refused as an unknown field.
- A supervisor stop stays blocked with its reason, and exits non-zero, whatever exit code its harness reports after the stop.
